**The accessibility prizes are a separate challenge**, not judged on the project: every team gets the same website and adds accessible features. Scored on keyboard and focus 25, visual 20, semantic HTML and screen readers 20, forms and interaction 15, testing and technical quality 20.

**Criteria mapped onto the kit's five**
- Innovation & Creativity 25 → Creativity
- Technical Complexity 25 → Technical
- Functionality & UX 20 → Design (and part of Technical: it has to work)
- Impact & Relevance 20 → Impact
- Presentation & Communication 10 → Presentation

**What kind of contest:** a small student event judged live at a table. Judges spend longer with each team, the live demo is the evidence, and story and polish count for more. The kit's questions about runs nobody started and a public live link mostly don't fit a live practice tool. Those sections get cut with a reason, or kept small.
