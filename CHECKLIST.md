# Hackathon checklist (from research, Oct 12, 2026)

Full notes: `C:\Users\sidhu\Documents\Sid's Life\Memory\reference_hackathon_winners_2026-10-12.md`

## Day 0 (about an hour)
- Read the live rules page. Write down: the deadline in Toronto time, the video's min and max length, the required sponsor tech, repo and licence rules, the pre-existing-work rule.
- Your three lines first: the main flow step by step, what one record holds, one trade-off you'd defend and its cost.
- One sentence: "For [a real person with a stake], X does [job] by [approach], so [outcome]."

## Build
- Put the sponsor's newest feature at the core, called in code. Name those files in the write-up (screening may read the code).
- One end-to-end path running by the halfway point. After that, improve it; don't extend it.
- Narrow build, broad proof: real data from two or more sources you didn't hand-pick.
- Your signature, shown early: the AI proposes, deterministic rules decide, it refuses with evidence.

## Submit
- About 15% of the time on one clean screen and the video.
- Video: who it's for, what it does and the sponsor tech in the first 20 seconds; the product on screen by about 0:20; one honest limitation; respect the length.
- Write-up: the one-line pitch first, one heading per judging criterion, a "where the sponsor tech is used" section, "measured" kept separate from "context".
- Claim every bonus (blog post, feedback, friction log). Declare any pre-existing code.
- Submit 12 to 24 hours early.
- Afterwards: explain it back in your own words.
